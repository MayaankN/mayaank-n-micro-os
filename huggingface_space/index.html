🧠 Mathematical Specialization: Dynamic Time Quantum Adaptation

The dynamic quantum algorithm continuously tunes each task's allocated time slice \(Q_{dyn}\) according to its historical CPU burst execution and the real-time depth of the ready queue:

$$Q_{dyn}(T) = \text{clamp}\left( Q_{base} + \text{sgn}(\text{burst}_{prev} - Q_{curr}) \cdot \Delta - \beta \cdot \max(0, |Q_{ready}| - 3), Q_{min}, Q_{max} \right)$$
⚡ CPU-bound Expansion

If a task exhausts its assigned quantum (\(\text{burst}_{avg} \ge Q\)), its slice expands (\(+1\)) up to \(Q_{max}\) (12 ticks). This reduces context-switch overhead and cache invalidation.

🎯 Interactive Compression

If a task yields early (\(\text{burst}_{avg} \le Q_{min}\)), its quantum contracts to \(Q_{min}\) (2 ticks), prioritizing snappy response times and lower latency for short bursts.

⚖️ Queue Pressure Relief

When the ready queue depth exceeds 3 waiting tasks, a load penalty scales down quanta to prevent long queue waiting times for arriving processes.
